Section 3

Kimball Star Schema Data Warehouse Design

Engineered for VertiPaq Columnar Compression in Power BI

Central Fact Table
FactSales
77,033 rows. Grain = individual POS transaction line item. Tracks Quantity, Amount, Cost, Profit, ReturnFlag.
Core Dimension Tables (1:* Relationships)
  • • DimStore: 8 Locations (Square footage, regional territory, monthly quotas)
  • • DimProduct: 40 SKUs across 5 Product Lines with cost & margin benchmarks
  • • DimDate: 366 Calendar days with ISO hierarchy & holiday surge flags
  • • DimCustomer: 1,200 Accounts segmented into VIP, Regular, Corporate, Student
  • • DimPromotion: 6 Marketing campaigns and discount thresholds

Section 5

Power BI Semantic Model & DAX Library

18+ Robust Calculated Measures Across 4 Key Domains

Core Financials & Pricing
  • • [Total Sales] = SUM(FactSales[SalesAmount])
  • • [Total Gross Profit] = [Total Sales] - [Total Cost]
  • • [Gross Margin %] = DIVIDE([Profit], [Sales], 0)
  • • [Average Order Value] = DIVIDE([Sales], [Orders], 0)
Time Intelligence & Spatial Productivity
  • • [Sales MoM Growth %] = DIVIDE(Current - PM, PM, 0)
  • • [Sales per Sq Ft] = DIVIDE([Sales], [Total SqFt], 0)
  • • [Sales YoY Growth %] = SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(...)
  • • [Target Attainment %] = DIVIDE([Sales], [Annual Quota], 0)
